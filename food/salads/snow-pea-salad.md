# Snow Pea Salad with Avocado

## Ingredients
* 8 ounces (225 grams) fresh snow peas
* 1/2 cup (50 grams) toasted walnuts (toast @ 350 degrees for 8-10 minutes)
* 4 tablespoons (60 ml) olive oil
* Juice of half a big lemon (1 1/2 tablespoons/20 ml)
* Kosher salt, to taste
* Freshly ground black pepper and mild red pepper flakes
* 1 avocado, halved, pitted, and sliced thin

## Directions
1. Soak snow peas in a large bowl of ice water for 10 to 20 minutes (longer is fine too). 
2. Drain and pat dry with a towel. 
3. Gather the peas in small stacks at a time and cut them lengthwise into thin ribbons.
4. Chop walnuts into rubble-sized pieces and place in a large bowl. 
5. Add olive oil, lemon juice, 1/2 to 1 teaspoon kosher salt (to taste), many grinds of black pepper, and pepper flakes to taste. Whisk to combine. 
6. Add snow peas and toss until the vegetables are coated. 
7. Add avocado and gently toss until combined. Eat right away.
